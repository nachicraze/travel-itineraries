🏂 Resort guide

Myoko Suginohara

Long, wide cruising runs with an 8.5 km longest course and powder zones. Most runs suit beginner/intermediate riders, though the full descent needs stamina. Good for the long-run day.

Official resort guide ↗

Ikenotaira Onsen

Wide, gentler beginner/intermediate terrain; a sensible choice for confidence-building and practicing turns.

Akakura Kanko / Akakura Onsen

Convenient village base with accommodation, restaurants and multiple slopes nearby. Offers groomed runs as well as powder terrain.

Seki Onsen

A small powder-focused area with more challenging terrain; better as an optional powder day than the main beginner learning base.

Suggested balance: Ikenotaira or easier Akakura runs for practice, then Suginohara for long scenic cruising when conditions and legs allow. Resort choice is not a confirmed booking.
