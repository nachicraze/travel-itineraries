📍 Resort highlights

Ikenotaira Onsen Alpen Blick

Wide, relatively gentle slopes make this a good confidence-building choice for beginners and riders who want to practise turns at a relaxed pace.

Myoko Suginohara

Known for long, wide cruising terrain and an 8.5 km longest run. Save this for a day when legs and conditions are good; the full descent takes stamina.

Akakura Onsen / Akakura Kanko

A lively ski village with restaurants and accommodation, plus a mix of groomed runs and powder options. A flexible choice for the third riding day.

Seki Onsen

A small, powder-focused area with more challenging terrain. Keep it as an optional alternative rather than the main beginner plan.

Suggested order: start with Ikenotaira for confidence, use Suginohara for the long-run day, then choose Akakura for a flexible final day. Check snow conditions, resort operations and lift-pass coverage before setting off.
