📍 Place highlights

Inawashiro Strawberry Farm

Greenhouse strawberry picking beside the roadside station, with views toward Mt. Bandai. A reservation is listed for 9 adults.

Genbikei Gorge

A scenic river gorge with sculpted rock formations and clear water. Check winter access before setting out.

Takkoku-no-iwaya Bishamondō

A Buddhist temple built against a dramatic rock face, making it a distinctive cultural stop.

Hōonji Temple · 500 Rakan

Known for its hall of carved Buddhist disciples; a quiet cultural stop on the way to Shizukuishi.

Koiwai Farm

A historic dairy farm with rural scenery, dairy treats and seasonal events such as winter illumination.

Lake Tazawa

A deep-blue caldera lake with the Tatsuko Statue, Gozanoishi Shrine and a lakeside rest stop.

Shizukuishi Ski Resort

Planned base for the first three snowboard days, with separate sightseeing and onsen time for Mum.

Nekoma Ski Resort

Planned for 27–28 Dec, replacing the earlier Grandeco cost assumption. Confirm seasonal lift and rental rates.

Urabandai

A lakeside base for smelt fishing, snowshoe trekking and winter scenery around the Goshikinuma area.
